Arcade controls

For the Arcade's Beach Buggy Racing 2: QtCarVehicle turns these into the game's steering wheel and scroll wheel devices while a game runs. Keys (click here first, or tick the box): ←→ / AD steer, ↓ / S / Space brake; a gamepad works too (left stick, B / left trigger).
For the MAME games: the steering wheel's scroll wheels (tilt left, press, tilt right, scroll), which QtCar turns into the game's keys (COIN and START are in its top bar).
