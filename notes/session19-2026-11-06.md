# Session 19 (2026-11-06): BeamNG.drive drives the UI (`--beamng`)

Goal: the UI shows the car in BeamNG.drive, as a part of its own next to tesla-can.py; also with the
game on another PC in the network. Only the user starts the game: nothing here has run against it.

## What the game offers (build 26298565, read from its `lua/vehicle/`)
- `protocols.lua` loads every `protocols/*.lua`: OutGauge and MotionSim off by default (both
  127.0.0.1:4444), anything else on by default (`protocols_others_enabled`). So a mod with one Lua
  file works without a setting. It only runs for the seated vehicle.
- After `fillStruct` it always sends, also when that returned early: a zeroed packet. Hence the magic.
- `udp:sendto` takes IP addresses only: `--target` resolves on the UI's PC and bakes in the IP.
- OutGauge has no steering, low beam, fog, hazards, doors, odometer. MotionSim only motion.
- Doors: `<name>_notAttached` of `advancedCouplerControl`: `door_FL_coupler` etc. (etk800), `door_L_coupler`
  (2 doors, covet), `tailgateCoupler`, `trunkCoupler`, `hoodLatchCoupler[LR]`, `hoodCatchCoupler`.
- `electrics.lua` latches its indicator stop on `steering > 60` with the left signal: positive = left.
- Tire pressure: `obj:getGroupPressure` in Pa with the atmosphere (`wheels.lua`). Game cars run far
  below the Model 3's 2.9 bar and would always warn (below 2.2), so the bridge scales to the pressure seen first.

## Design
tesla-can.py's control port, as `tesla-gps.py --can`, with two small additions: `quiet` in `set` (no
log line at 20 Hz) and `car` in `info`. Every SIM_ value is an HTTP request in tesla-can.py, and
these servers keep sockets: SIM_ numbers at most once a second. Details: `beamng/README.md`.

## Tested
- `beamng/test_bridge.py`: 25 tests, including every name and enum label against both databases, and
  the mod's real `fillStruct` under luajit with stubs of the game's globals.
- Sandbox, both firmwares, bridge on 0.0.0.0 and `fake-game.py` sending to the PC's LAN address
  (doesn't pass a firewall, traffic to a local address stays local). In QtCarVehicle's values: rail, gear,
  speed, odometer, battery, current, signals, hazards, lights, parking brake, doors, trunk, frunk, outside
  temperature, tires, steering (Model 3); LOC_geoLocation follows. On the Model 3's screen: 56 mph, D, the
  left signal, opened door/trunk/frunk, 25 psi in orange. Game gone: P, 0.
- The sandbox's S/X cluster says "Car Off" with or without the bridge (control: tesla-can.py's `drive`
  preset, also Car Off), so the cluster's speed wasn't seen.
- Stock OutGauge + MotionSim through a real tesla-can.py: gear, speed, rail, battery; no invented doors.

## First drive in the game (the user's run, same day)
The mod and the bridge worked. Speed was too low: 250 km/h showed as about 150. QtCar shows
`DI_uiSpeedHighSpeed` as it is under the label of the UI's own setting (checked on the running UI:
155 + MPH under `GUI_distanceUnits=Kilometers` shows "155 KM/H", 250 + KPH shows "250 KM/H" and
`VAPI_vehicleSpeed` stays 155.3 mph). The real inverter follows the UI; the bridge now does too,
polling `GUI_distanceUnits` on :4220 every 2 s. Tesla-can's panel Speed row still sends mph.

Rear camera survey: the game's camera sensor (`createCameraSensor*`) needs a BeamNG.tech licence;
`RenderViewManagerInstance` renders a second view for Lua, but only to the screen. So the picture
has to come from capturing the game's window, into `--camera`'s v4l2loopback. Not built yet.

Brake lights: the driving view lights its car from `LIGHT_brake*`, which come from `VC{LEFT,RIGHT}_brakeLightStatus`, not from the pedal (`VCLEFT_brakePressed`). Checked on the running UI. Tail lamps likewise (`VC*_tailLightStatus` -> `LIGHT_tail*`).
